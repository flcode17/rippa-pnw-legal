Privacy Policy

Rippa PNW Metrics Sync · Effective date: July 26, 2026

Overview

Rippa PNW Metrics Sync is an internal application operated by Best Seedling LLC (doing business as Rippa PNW) to connect our own QuickBooks Online company to a private, internal business-metrics dashboard. This application is not published or made available to other companies or the general public — it connects only to our own QuickBooks Online account.

What data we access

Through the QuickBooks Online API, this application reads (and never writes):

  • Customer records — name, contact information, and account balance
  • Sales transactions — invoices, sales receipts, credit memos, and refund receipts, including line-item detail (products/services, quantities, and amounts)

How we use this data

Data is used solely to generate internal sales and business-performance charts for company management. It is not used for marketing, advertising, or any purpose outside of internal reporting.

Data storage and security

Synced data is stored in a private database accessible only to authorized company personnel. Authentication credentials used to connect to QuickBooks are stored securely and are never exposed publicly. The connection to QuickBooks is read-only — this application never creates, modifies, or deletes data in QuickBooks.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share this data with any third party.

Data retention

Data is retained only for as long as needed to operate the internal dashboard. It is deleted upon request or when the application is decommissioned.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of this application after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
